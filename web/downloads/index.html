First time opening on macOS?

Because Nook is an independent application distributed directly from its website rather than through the Mac App Store, macOS Gatekeeper may present a security prompt on first launch: “Apple could not verify the developer of Nook” or “Nook was downloaded from the internet”.

Safest Method: In Finder, navigate to your Applications folder. Control-click (or right-click) Nook.app, choose Open from the menu, and then click Open in the dialog. You only need to do this once.
System Settings Method: Alternatively, open System Settings  > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click Open Anyway.
